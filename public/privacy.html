PRIVACY

How Rashcuts may handle personal information, browser storage and booking/contact data.

1 / What this policy covers

This policy explains how Rashcuts may handle personal information you choose to provide while using the public website, booking/contact channels, and related digital services. It is intended to operate alongside applicable Nigerian data-protection law, including the Nigeria Data Protection Act 2023.

2 / Information we may receive

Depending on how you use the site, this can include your name, phone number, email address, service selections, preferred appointment date/time, private-session location information that you choose to provide, booking notes, and messages you send to us. The site may also use necessary browser storage for interface state and cached site configuration.

3 / Why we use it

We may use information to respond to enquiries, arrange or administer appointments, understand the services you selected, provide private-session logistics, maintain the website, secure administrative access, prevent abuse, and comply with legal obligations. We aim to collect only what is reasonably necessary for the purpose.

4 / Booking & third-party channels

The current public site can prepare booking information in your browser and hand the final conversation to a third-party channel such as WhatsApp. Once you leave the Rashcuts website, that third party processes information under its own terms and privacy policy. Please avoid sending sensitive information that is not needed to arrange your appointment.

5 / Storage, security & retention

We use reasonable technical and organisational measures to protect information against unauthorised access, loss, misuse or disclosure. Personal data should be retained only for as long as reasonably necessary for the purpose for which it was collected, legitimate business needs, dispute handling, fraud prevention, or legal obligations. Administrative content such as prices, images and location settings may be stored in the studio's private business database and media storage.

6 / Cookies & local storage

The current site is designed to minimise browser tracking. It may use local storage and service-worker caching for functional state and performance. This is different from placing a marketing cookie, and users can clear browser storage through their browser controls. Third-party services linked from the site may use their own cookies or tracking technologies.

7 / Your rights

Subject to applicable law and any lawful exceptions, you may have rights to be informed about processing, access your personal data, request correction, object to certain processing, request restriction or erasure, withdraw consent where consent is the basis, request portability where applicable, and complain to the Nigeria Data Protection Commission.

Requests should include enough information for Rashcuts to identify the relevant record and verify the requester where necessary.

8 / Children

The site is not intentionally designed to collect personal data from children for independent use. Where a booking involves a minor, the responsible parent or guardian should handle the booking and provide only information necessary for the appointment.

9 / International & third-party processing

Some website infrastructure or third-party services may process information outside Nigeria. Where that occurs, Rashcuts will seek to use providers and safeguards appropriate to the nature of the information and applicable law. Third-party services remain governed by their own terms.

10 / Updates & contact

We may update this policy when our services, systems, or legal obligations change. The effective date below identifies the current version. For a privacy request or complaint, use the booking/contact channel on the main site and state that your request is a privacy matter.

Effective date: 28 September 2026

This policy is a practical website-policy template, not legal advice. Rashcuts should have the final version reviewed by a Nigerian privacy lawyer/Data Protection Compliance Organisation (DPCO) where appropriate, particularly before the site begins storing significant customer personal data in its administrative database.
